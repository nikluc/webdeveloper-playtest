WebDeveloper

You are a spider. Hunt and build your webs by day, then get your spiderlings safely out by night.
This is an early playtest: rough edges and placeholder art are expected.

Play in browser

Needs a desktop or laptop with keyboard and mouse, in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. The first load takes a little while. Click the game once to give it focus.

What we want you to try

Play one full day and one night (about 10 minutes), then tell us how it felt.

  1. Build your Nest. Press B, then 2, click the three highlighted pebbles near where you start, and press Enter. An egg sac appears inside.
  2. Feed the egg sac. Build webs (B, 1, click 3+ anchor spots, Enter), wrap the flies they catch, carry the food home and drop it in the Nest. The more it eats, the more spiderlings hatch tonight.
  3. At midday a pale dashed trail appears: tonight your spiderlings will walk it to a tall plant. Webs built across it will help protect them.
  4. At night the spiderlings walk the glowing trail. Angry flies try to snatch them and beetles block the way. Chase them off and rescue the little ones.
  5. At dawn you see how many made it out. Escaped spiderlings give you XP.

Good to know

  • Webs need anchor spots: rocks, trees and tall grass.
  • Building and repairing cost silk. Eating food gives silk back.
  • If the food count turns red, the egg sac is starving: bring food home.
  • Press F1 in the game to see the instructions again.
  • Your progress is not saved yet. Closing the tab starts a new garden.
